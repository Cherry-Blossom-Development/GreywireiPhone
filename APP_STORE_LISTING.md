## Description
Greywire Studio is the companion app for Linework series guitar pedals.

Connect your phone to Switchyard, the control pedal at the heart of your board. Set up sounds and setlists here, and Switchyard changes every Linework pedal with one tap.

FEATURES
• Sign in with your Greywire account (same as Prosaurus)
• View your Greywire membership status
• See your claimed pedals
• More features coming as the Linework series develops

ABOUT GREYWIRE STUDIO
Greywire Studio designs guitar effects that work together. The Linework series starts with Flashover (analog distortion) and Switchyard (the control pedal), with more to come.
